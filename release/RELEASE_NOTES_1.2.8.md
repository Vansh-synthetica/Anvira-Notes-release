### Changed
- **Sign in to use Anvira Notes.** The app now opens with a sign-in screen (or **Create an account**, free). Your notes and files still stay on this PC. With **Remember me** (on by default) you sign in once: the app opens straight away after that, offline too, and your session renews itself in the background, after sleep and when the internet comes back. You are only asked again if you sign out, turn Remember me off, or the session is ended from your account. If a confirmation email does not arrive, **Send it again** is right there.

### Better
- **Updates say what they bring.** Clicking **Update available** now shows what is new and how big the download is before anything downloads, then the progress in megabytes and speed. A failed download stays visible on the button with **Try again**, instead of only in a tooltip. After restarting, the app tells you it was updated.
- **Runtime updates are clearer.** The size shown now includes the GPU pack when your runtime has it (it used to say about 49 MB when about 700 MB would download), the button shows which part is downloading, and an error stays on the button instead of disappearing.
- **Smoother everywhere.** Dialogs in Study and across the app now fade and rise into place like those in Notes; flashcards slide in and flip when you turn them over; each new quiz, practice and mock exam question settles in; answers give a small response when picked; study screens slide up into view. All of it is light (nothing re-lays out the page) and switches off with Windows' "Show animations" setting.
- The title bar shows just **Anvira Notes**.

### Fixed
- The **X** (and other buttons near the top) did nothing in flashcards, quizzes and other study screens opened from a notebook's Guide: the window-drag strip underneath swallowed the click.
