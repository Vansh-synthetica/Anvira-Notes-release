### Better
- **One look across the app.** Messages, Settings, dialogs, the chat pop-ups and Study's dark mode now share the same graphite as Notes, with one blue accent, so moving between sections feels like one app. Study keeps its light look by default.
- **A new start-up screen:** the LocalHouseLLM mark draws itself, with Anvira Notes beneath it.
- **Messages are more private and more robust.** Each person's updates now travel on their own private channel, and typing goes over a channel only the chat's members can join, so nobody receives anything that isn't theirs. Long chat histories page back without skipping or repeating a message, a message that fails to send says why, and a chat catches up on reactions and read receipts after the connection drops.
- **Manage your profile on the web.** Settings → Account, the account menu and the Messages profile window have **Manage profile**, which opens your profile on localhousellm.com. Changes appear in the app when you come back to it.
