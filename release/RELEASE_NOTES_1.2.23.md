### Shared things can be opened now
What you share in a chat now comes with the thing itself, not just its title.
- **Pages:** **Open** to read it, **Save to my notes** to keep it in any notebook. Whiteboards and tables can be shared too, not only text pages.
- **Lessons:** **Study these cards** (or **Take this quiz**) saves the lesson into your notes and starts it straight away, with every card new for you.
- **Mock exams:** share one from the exam results or the grades list. Classmates get the questions to sit, never your answers or marks. **Take this exam** starts it.
- **Notebooks:** share a whole notebook from its menu (or the chat icon next to it in the sidebar). **Save notebook** adds it, with its pages and lessons.

Shared items stay private to the chat, like its photos and files.

### Pochi in your chats
Click ✨ at the top of a chat. It runs on the AI on your PC, and the chat never leaves it.
- **Catch me up:** what you missed, in a few lines: what was decided, what people asked you, dates, and what was shared.
- **Turn this chat into notes:** a page of study notes from what the group worked out, with a to-do list, saved to the notebook you choose.

Pochi only writes what the chat says: lines with numbers, dates or tasks the chat never had are left out. Tested on two study-group chats with Qwen2.5-Coder 3B (14 answers in all): no made-up numbers, dates or formulas got through, and once a catch-up line mixed up who would do a task. Each answer took 1–3 seconds on a GPU.

### Messages
- **Light mode:** Messages turns light when Notes is light (Settings → Notes → Light editor).
- **Shared in this chat:** chat info now lists every page, notebook, lesson and exam shared there, ready to open, then photos and files.
- **Small animations:** shared cards, menus, chat info and dialogs move a little when they appear, and Pochi shimmers while it reads. They are off when Windows is set to show fewer animations.
- Long group names end in "…" in the pop-up chat instead of being cut off.

Someone on an older Anvira Notes sees a shared item as a card with a file attached. They can open it once they update.

### Update
If you have an earlier Anvira Notes, it offers this update by itself: click **Update available** at the top right, then **Restart to update**. Or download Anvira-Notes-Setup-1.2.23.exe below. Your notes, lessons and settings are kept. Anvira Runtime does not need an update.
