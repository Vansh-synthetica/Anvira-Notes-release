### New in Messages
- **Photos and files.** Attach them with the paperclip, paste a screenshot, or drag files onto a chat. Photos show in the conversation and open full-size (← → between them); files download with one click. Up to 10 at a time, 25 MB each. They are private to the chat: only its members can see them.
- **Formatting.** **\*\*bold\*\***, *\*italic\**, ~strike~, `` `code` `` and code blocks, with Ctrl+B and Ctrl+I.
- **@mentions in groups.** Type @ to pick someone. Mentions of you are highlighted. A muted chat stays muted, mentions included.
- **Search a chat** with Ctrl+F or the search button, including older messages. Enter jumps through the matches.
- **"New messages" line** where you left off.
- **"Active now"** when the other person has the chat open (members only, never stored).
- **Drafts are kept per chat** and shown in the chat list, so switching chats never loses what you were typing.
- **Chat list tabs** (All, Unread, Groups) and **pinned chats** at the top.
- **Small touches.** Double-click a message for a quick ❤️, forward a message to another chat, see who has read it in groups ("Seen by 3"), and hear a soft chime for new messages (Settings → Messages → Sound).

### Profiles
- **Profile photos** set on localhousellm.com show everywhere: chats, the pop-up chats, member lists and your own button in the sidebar.
- **Profile changes arrive at once**, for you and everyone you chat with.
- A direct chat's info panel shows the other person's bio, school and website.
