# Anvira Notes 1.2.35

A brand new Pochi Club, and a smoother, more satisfying Notes.

## Pochi Club, redesigned
- A new console-style home for Pochi: shelves along the bottom, Pochi on her stage, and a big card for whatever you pick. Drive it all with the arrow keys, or click.
- **Wardrobe categories**: Hats & bows, Glasses, Face, Outfits, Bags & wings and Auras, plus All. Each shows how many you own. Sort by shelf, price or what you own.
- **Try before you buy**: try on as many things as you like, even ones you can't buy yet. Keep the look with one **Buy all** using your Tuna Coins, or clear it.
- **Pochi really moves now**: every move is acted out with her fins, antenna, feet and eyes, with squash and stretch, and little pixel hearts, notes, stars, dust and confetti.
- Quests and the wallet have their own shelves.
- Light mode: the club follows Study's light and dark setting.

## Notes
- The editor toolbar stays on one row and tucks tools away neatly as the window gets narrower.
- Menus and dropdowns fade in and out, and work with the arrow keys and Enter.
- Arrow keys move through the sidebar and the page tree.
- Optional tap sounds (Settings → Sounds): soft **Felt** or **Pebble**, with a volume slider.

## Fixed
- The AI edit panel no longer shows a bare `{}` instead of an answer.
- The sidebar now opens over the labs and the Pochi Club.
