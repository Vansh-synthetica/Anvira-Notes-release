# Anvira Notes 1.2.37

Lessons are ready sooner.

## Study
- **Prepare lessons while idle.** When you're away from the PC, Anvira quietly writes flashcards and quiz questions for the notebooks you changed recently. Later, **Make a lesson** finds most of the work already done. Flashcards then take about a second instead of several, and a quiz takes about half as long.
- It's built to go easy on your PC:
  - It runs only when the whole PC has been idle for a while, it's plugged in, and the model runs on a graphics card. It never runs on battery or when the model is on the CPU.
  - It works in short bursts with long rests in between, so the graphics card stays cool and quiet. Busy programs make the rests longer.
  - It stops at once when you touch the keyboard or mouse, unplug, or the PC gets hot. Work it already finished is kept.
  - Each notebook is prepared once per change.
- You can turn it off in **Settings → Study → Prepare lessons while idle**.

## With Anvira Runtime 1.0.3
On an NVIDIA graphics card, the runtime now writes repetitive text, like flashcards, up to about 1.7× faster. It does this by reusing phrases that already appear in the notes and in its own answer. Answers come out the same, and on a CPU nothing changes. Click **Runtime update** at the top right to get it. Anvira Notes 1.2.37 also works with Runtime 1.0.2.

If you have an earlier Anvira Notes, it offers this update by itself: click **Update available** at the top right, then **Restart to update**. Or download Anvira-Notes-Setup-1.2.37.exe below. Your notes, lessons and settings are kept.
