## Anvira Notes 1.0.1

### New
- **Today's session.** One tap in Study starts a short daily lesson that mixes your due flashcards and quiz questions from every notebook, Duolingo-style. Questions you miss come back at the end, and every answer keeps your review schedule right.
- **Reviews coming up.** A 7-day forecast in Study shows how many cards are due each day.
- **Trash.** Deleting a page moves it (with its sub-pages) to the trash, with **Undo** right away. Restore it any time in the next 30 days from the bottom of the sidebar.
- **Favourites and recents.** Star a page to pin it at the top of the sidebar; your recently opened pages sit there too, across all notebooks.
- **Duplicate and move pages.** Copy a page with its sub-pages, or move it to another notebook (page menu **⋯**).
- **Import files as pages.** Bring in Word, PowerPoint, Excel, OpenDocument, PDF, Markdown, web pages (.html), CSV or text: **New → Import file…**. Headings, lists, checklists, tables and quotes come across.
- **Word export.** **Download → Word (.docx)** with real headings, nested and numbered lists, checklists, tables, callouts, code, links, highlights, images, and charts and diagrams as pictures. Opens in Word, Google Docs and Pages.

### Fixed
- Charts and diagrams follow the light and dark themes.
- Diagrams in PDF and Word exports render as clean pictures.
- Opening a favourite whose page is in the trash now says so instead of opening a different page.

### Update
If you have Anvira Notes 1.0.0, it offers this update by itself: click **Update available** at the top right, then **Restart to update**. Or download **Anvira-Notes-Setup-1.0.1.exe** below. Your notes, lessons and settings are kept.
