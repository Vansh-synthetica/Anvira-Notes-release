### New
- **Pictures you can place anywhere.** Click a picture and drag a corner to resize it (its shape is kept). A small bar over the picture sets where it sits: left, center or right, picture on the left or right with text flowing beside it, or fit to the page width. Drag the picture itself to move it anywhere on the page.
- **Pictures reach Google Docs and Notion.** Google Docs gets the size and alignment (it has no wrapped pictures, so a picture with text beside it goes there aligned to the same side). Pictures from your PC now reach Notion too — before, they stayed in Anvira only. Notion has no picture sizes or alignment, so those stay in Anvira, and nothing you set is lost when a page syncs back.
- **Study plans that cover the whole book.** Pick your exam date: Anvira reads every PDF and source in the notebook and lays out a daily path to the exam. Click a day to get that day's flashcards, quiz and practice problems, made only when you open it (faster, lighter). Finished days come off the checklist, so tomorrow continues with new material instead of repeating.
- **Live cursors.** See where others are typing (a caret with their name) and where their mouse is on the page, like in Figma.
- **"Create notebooks automatically" setting.** Settings now lets you turn automatic notebook creation on or off.

### Better
- **Mind maps.** Bigger, more useful maps when the material needs it, with labels that fit their boxes.
- **Ask Pochi** keeps the context of the conversation in follow-up questions.

### Fixed
- **Who's here.** The presence bar no longer shows wrong or duplicate names.
- **Whiteboard:** the corner buttons no longer overlap.
- **Google Docs:** a picture added above a paragraph no longer ends up inside that paragraph; deleting the last paragraph no longer leaves an empty line; pictures already in the document are no longer uploaded again on every sync; one picture failing to upload no longer stops the rest of the page from syncing.
- **Notion:** pages with a table now settle after a sync instead of always looking changed.
- The AI writing bar no longer pops up over a selected picture.
