## Anvira Notes 1.0.3

### New
- **Record a lecture.** Press **Record** (next to Ask AI) and Anvira Notes writes down what's said as it's said, on your PC, in English or other languages including Hindi. When you stop, **Turn into notes** gives you a page with a summary, key points, key terms, formulas, examples, review questions, homework as a checklist, and the full transcript. The audio is saved in Documents › Anvira Notes › Recordings.
- **Ask Pochi from "/".** Type `/` and then anything that isn't a block name (`/explain the Krebs cycle`, `/make 5 quiz questions`) and Pochi writes it straight into your page, using what's above as context.
- **Google Docs without signing in.** Paste the link of any document shared as "Anyone with the link" and it opens view-only, kept up to date, with Google's own formatting and pictures. Press **Edit here** to switch to two-way editing.
- **See who's on the page.** On a Google Doc or Notion page, everyone who has it open in Anvira Notes shows up by name (or an anonymous name like "Anonymous Otter" if they aren't signed in), including who is typing.
- **Web research that works.** Ask AI and Study chat's **Web Research** now search the web and Wikipedia, read the pages and answer with numbered sources.

### Better
- **Google Docs look like Google Docs.** Fonts, sizes, colours, highlights, alignment, spacing, indents, the document's own heading styles and list numbering (1. a. i.) come across, and changes to colour, size, font and alignment go back.
- **Anvira Runtime is steadier.** If the AI model stops, it's restarted and your request is retried automatically; requests made while a model is still loading now wait instead of failing.
- **Links:** the Link button and **Ctrl+K** open a small link box (they did nothing before).
- **Search in the sidebar** now finds pages in every notebook, by title or by the words inside them.
- Menus close after you pick something or press Escape; Escape also closes page history, dialogs and pickers.

### Fixed
- Google Docs lists were shown as checkboxes.
- The "/" menu kept disappearing on pages synced with Google Docs.
- The installer ignored the folder you chose, could crash while choosing one, and didn't list the app in Settings › Apps.
- Study screens (whiteboard, reviews, quizzes, the notebook studio) were partly hidden under the side bar, which covered their Back button.
- The Study whiteboard opened the mind map instead of your whiteboard.
- A study plan with nothing to schedule now says so and offers to make flashcards or a quiz.
- Downloads and "Continue writing" now tell you when something goes wrong instead of doing nothing.

### Update
If you have an earlier Anvira Notes, it offers this update by itself: click **Update available** at the top right, then **Restart to update**. Or download **Anvira-Notes-Setup-1.0.3.exe** below. Your notes, lessons and settings are kept.
