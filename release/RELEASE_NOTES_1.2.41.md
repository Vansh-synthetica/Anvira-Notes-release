# Anvira Notes 1.2.41

Try a new, Mac-style look for Anvira. It's a preview: switch it on or off whenever you like.

## New: Mac-style look (preview)
Turn it on in **Settings → Look → Mac-style look (preview)**. It's off until you switch it on, and turning it off puts everything back exactly as it was.

- **Cleaner type:** the app uses Inter, a crisp font close to the one on Apple devices, with tighter large titles.
- **One sidebar:** the section rail and the Notes sidebar share one tone that runs to the top of the window, and the top bar blends into the toolbar under it, like a Mac app.
- **Calmer greys:** slightly cooler dark greys and thin dividers instead of boxes. Sidebar headings read "Recent" and "Pages" instead of capitals.
- **Smooth section switching:** the highlight glides from Notes to Study to Messages instead of jumping.
- **Settings like System Settings:** each section has its own coloured icon.

The first time you switch it on, Anvira downloads the Inter font (a small file). Until then, and when you're offline, it uses the usual font.

Tell us what you think with **Report a problem** in the account menu: we'd like to know whether it should become the default.
