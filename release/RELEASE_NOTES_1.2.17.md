### A new Study home
- **Pochi's desk.** Study now opens with Pochi telling you what matters today (cards due, your next lesson, a mock exam waiting) above a real ask box. Type a question and press Enter, and the chat opens with it already asked. Shortcuts for today's session, studying from a file, flashcards, quizzes and explanations sit right below.
- **Calmer layout.** Today's session, your notebooks and the Chemistry Lab, Physics Lab and mock exams are now quiet cards instead of a stack of bright banners. Each notebook shows its progress and its next lesson.
- **English or Tagalog in one click.** The Study header has an EN / TL switch.

### Inside a notebook
- Open a notebook to see its lessons as a path, with Pochi standing on the next one and an **Up next** card to start it. Long paths open scrolled to where you are.
- Beside the path: today's review for that notebook, **Ask about this notebook** (chat, study guide and whiteboard from its notes), its weak spots, and buttons to make more lessons.
- Every lesson still opens with Start, a timed exam, share, Anki export, edit and delete.

### Update
