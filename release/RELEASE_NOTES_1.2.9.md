### New
- **Messages** (in the sidebar, or **Ctrl 3**). Talk to classmates and run group projects without leaving Anvira.
  - **Direct chats and groups.** Find someone by their **@username** or their exact email address, or share a group's **invite code** so people can join with it. Free groups hold up to 15 people.
  - **A real messenger.** Replies (click a reply to jump to the original), emoji reactions, edit and delete your own messages, links you can click, a typing bubble, **Seen** in direct chats, big emoji, and the full history as you scroll up. **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** adds a line, **↑** edits your last message, **Esc** cancels, and the 👍 button sends a quick thumbs up.
  - **Chats pop up while you work.** Like Messenger on Facebook: a message that arrives while you are writing notes or studying opens a small chat at the bottom right, so you can read it and answer without switching. Your typing is never interrupted. Click its title to shrink it to a round bubble, open it in Messages, or close it. Up to three chats sit side by side, and the rest wait as bubbles with an unread count and a short preview. During a timed mock exam, chats only wait as bubbles. Prefer a bubble over an open chat, or no pop-ups at all? Choose in **Settings → Messages**, or pick **Turn off chat pop-ups** from a chat's **⋯** menu.
  - **Share your work into a chat.** From a page's menu in Notes (**Share to a chat**) or a lesson in Study (the chat button on its card), a classmate gets a card with its title and contents.
  - **Stays out of your way.** An unread count on the sidebar, desktop notifications only while Anvira is in the background (they can be turned off), mute per chat, block someone from the chat's info panel, and report a message with the flag beside it.
  - **Your profile:** a name, an @username, a colour, and whether people can find you by @username (if you turn that off, only people who know your exact email or share a group with you can reach you).
  - Messages are stored on Anvira's server so they reach the other person. Your notes and files still stay on this PC.
- **Anvira Pro is on its way.** Settings now shows your plan, and **See Pro** explains what Pro will add (cloud AI for exams and marking, weak-spot re-tests and exam packs, bigger groups, encrypted sync). **Get early access** puts you on the list. Nothing is charged, and everything that runs on your PC stays free.
