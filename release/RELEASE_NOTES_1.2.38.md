# Anvira Notes 1.2.38

Recording the PC audio works again.

## Record a lecture
- **PC audio** and **Both** work again. Since 1.2.32 the installed app turned down its own request to hear the PC, so these modes showed "Error starting capture", even without any antivirus. The microphone was never affected.
- Recording the PC audio no longer captures the screen behind the scenes. Only the sound is taken.
- In **Both**, your microphone is turned down while the PC is playing, so the lecture coming out of your speakers is not transcribed twice. Ask your questions in the pauses, or use headphones.
- If the PC audio can't be recorded, you're told straight away when you press record, not after the speech model has loaded.

Everything is still transcribed on your PC.
