# Anvira Notes 1.2.33

Ready for whole classes: signing in from a busy school network, and Messages for many students at once.

## Signing in at school
- When lots of people sign in or create accounts on the same school network at once, the app now waits and tries again by itself, and says so ("Lots of people are signing in on this network…"), instead of failing with "Too many attempts".
- A busy network no longer signs you out: if keeping you signed in is refused for a moment, the app tries again a minute or two later.

## Messages
- Messages connects live only while a chat is on screen (in Messages, or a chat open at the bottom right). In Notes and Study the app holds no connection, so many more students can use Anvira at the same time.
- After 5 minutes in the background or 10 minutes away, the live connection closes, and opens again as soon as you are back.
- In between, new messages are checked every minute (every 3 minutes in the background): unread badges, chat pop-ups and notifications still arrive.
