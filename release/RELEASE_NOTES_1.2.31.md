# Anvira Notes 1.2.31

Lecture and meeting recording got a big upgrade, including transcripts that know who is speaking.

## Who said what
- New "Several people talking" option: put your PC in the middle of a study group or meeting, and after Stop the transcript is split by speaker.
- Each person gets a colour and a name ("Speaker 1", "Speaker 2"…). Click a name to rename it.
- The saved page shows how many people spoke and for how long, and the transcript reads "[0:06] Rika: …" turn by turn.
- "Turn into notes" knows who asked and who answered.
- If it heard the wrong number of people, choose how many and press "Label again".
- Runs entirely on this PC. The speaker model (54 MB) downloads once, in the background while you record. The temporary audio it reads is deleted when the recorder closes.

## Better transcripts
- Silence, fan noise and music no longer turn into made-up text like "Thank you.", and quiet stretches are skipped faster.
- Quiet or distant speakers (the back of a lecture hall, a soft video) are no longer dropped, and noisy rooms are cut at real sentence breaks.
- Choose which microphone to use; the choice is remembered.
- New "Both" source: PC audio and your microphone together, for an online class where you speak too.
- A hint appears when nothing has been heard for a while (muted mic, wrong mic, paused video).

## Notes
- Drop files straight onto Sources in the sidebar.
- Language menus follow the dark or light theme, and dropdowns open with a small animation.
