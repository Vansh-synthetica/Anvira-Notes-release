# Anvira Notes 1.2.40

Smoother everywhere: fewer flashes, no blank starts and clearer AI states.

## Everywhere
- Switching between Study and Notes, Messages or Settings no longer flashes grey on the way.
- The app opens faster: the start-up animation now takes about a second instead of two.
- Empty screens (no notebooks, no chats yet) ease in instead of popping up, and loading spinners fade in gently. A quick load no longer flickers.

## Notes
- Anvira reopens the page you were on last, instead of a blank new page.
- When a notebook takes a moment to open, a quiet placeholder appears instead of the empty draft.
- In the sidebar, Recent shows which notebook a page is in when two pages have the same name.
- With Ask AI open on a laptop screen, the toolbar stays on one row, so the page no longer jumps down. The list buttons are also in **Format → Lists**.

## Ask AI
- While the AI is starting or a model is still loading, Ask AI says so ("Waiting for … to load") instead of showing "…".
- When the AI can't answer, you get the reason in plain words with **Try again** and **AI settings** buttons. Before, it showed "Error: …" as if it were an answer, with an "Insert into page" button. Stopping an answer now just says "Stopped."
- The Study chat explains AI problems in plain words too.
