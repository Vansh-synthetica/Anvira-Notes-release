### Study fixes
- **No more off-topic questions.** Quizzes and flashcards could include questions that had nothing to do with your notes (Earth's mantle in a biology lesson). Every question and card is now checked against the part of your material it was written from, and anything that is not about it is left out.
- **Every file is used.** With several files or pages selected, a lesson could come only from the longest one. Now each one is read, and the lesson takes its questions and cards evenly from all of them.
