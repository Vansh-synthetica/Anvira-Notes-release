## Anvira Notes 1.0.2: work with Google Docs and Notion

### New
- **Open any Google Doc or Notion page here.** **New → Google Doc or Notion page…**: paste its link, or browse Google Drive / Notion. It opens as a page you edit in Anvira Notes.
- **Live, both ways.** Your edits reach the original a few seconds after you stop typing, and everyone else's edits appear here while the page is open. Only what changed is edited, so collaborators' work is never overwritten; if two people change the same paragraph at the same moment, both versions are kept.
- **See who is working on it.** The bar under the title shows who has been editing and when.
- **Send to Google Docs / Send to Notion.** Turn any page into a document you can share with anyone (page menu **⋯**). It stays in sync with your page.
- **Settings → Connections.** Connect your Google account or Notion workspace. You sign in on Google's or Notion's own page in your browser; your password never touches this app, and it only sees the documents you open with it.
- Copied a Google Docs or Notion link? Come back to Anvira Notes and it offers to open it.

### Good to know
- Charts, diagrams and toggles stay in Anvira Notes when syncing with Google Docs; pictures added from your PC stay here too (Google and Notion only accept pictures from the web).
- Notion databases, sub-pages and embeds stay in Notion, untouched.

### Update
If you have Anvira Notes 1.0.0 or 1.0.1, it offers this update by itself: click **Update available** at the top right, then **Restart to update**. Or download **Anvira-Notes-Setup-1.0.2.exe** below. Your notes, lessons and settings are kept.
