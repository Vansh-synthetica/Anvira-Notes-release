### Study items stay on your notes
- **No more questions about the wrong subject.** With small AI models, a lesson could include questions or cards about a different subject than your notes (the solar system in a biology lesson, chlorophyll in a history lesson). The examples Pochi learns the format from were pulling the model toward their own topic. They now use made-up names that no real notes contain, so anything copied from them is always caught and left out.
- **Every item is checked against your notes,** including practice problems, mock-exam questions and mind maps, not only quizzes and flashcards.
- **Tagalog lessons from English notes are checked too.** Each Tagalog card or question must share a term, name or number with your notes, so off-topic or garbled ones are left out.
- **Study guides, cheat sheets, mind maps and mock-exam explanations** now stick to what your notes say instead of adding outside facts.
- In our tests with small local models, off-topic items reaching the student went from 7 to 0, and the 3B model now keeps more good Tagalog flashcards.
