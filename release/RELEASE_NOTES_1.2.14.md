### Chemistry Lab fixes
- **The sidebar no longer covers the lab.** The Chemistry Lab now fits beside the sidebar, so the back button and the 3D/Lewis switch are always visible, and you can still switch sections from the sidebar.
- **The 3D view follows the theme.** It was white in dark mode; it now matches the dark (and light) theme.
- **Molecules open properly framed.** A new molecule no longer appears tiny or zoomed far in: it opens from a three-quarter angle with every atom in view, and fits itself again when the window or the view changes size (until you move it yourself).
