# Anvira Notes 1.2.34

New Electricity and Magnetism sections in the Physics Lab.

## Physics Lab: Electricity
- Ohm's law: set the resistance directly, or build it from a real wire (length, thickness, material). An ammeter and a voltmeter read along, next to the I–V graph.
- Coulomb's law with charges you can drag around.
- Electric field and potential.
- Series and parallel circuits: click a resistor to burn it out and see what happens to the others.
- Power and energy, including working out your electricity bill.
- Capacitors and RC charging.
- Kirchhoff's laws: walk each loop and watch the voltages add to zero.
- Circuit builder: wires, resistors, bulbs, batteries, switches and capacitors, with ready-made circuits to start from.

## Physics Lab: Magnetism
- The magnetic field round a long wire, a solenoid and a moving charge.
- The force on a wire in a field, two parallel wires, and a rod sliding through a field (how a generator works).

Every simulation shows its formulas with your numbers, has worked examples with a **Try it** button, and works in Tagalog.
