### New: Pochi Club
Studying now earns **Tuna Coins** for Pochi. Open Pochi Club from the little coin badge under Pochi on the Study page.

- **Buddy:** name Pochi, pick a mood and a home (Cloud nook, Moon garden, Jelly lagoon, Stargazer room), say hello and play a routine. Friendship grows each day you study and say hello, and memories keep track of your milestones.
- **Shop:** 20 accessories (hats, glasses, outfits, bags, auras), 6 colours and 3 animation packs. Hover an item to try it on before you buy.
- **Moves:** 8 moves that unlock as you study, plus 15 more in the packs. Choose Pochi's favourite move and a two-move routine.
- **Quests:** small daily goals (say good morning, finish a lesson, reach your daily goal, give Pochi a new look) with a few extra coins.
- **Wallet:** your balance and every coin earned and spent.

**How coins come in:** 1 coin for every 4 XP from lessons, flashcards, quizzes and practice; +40 for reaching your daily goal; +30 for a mock exam; streak bonuses at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 100 days; and 120 to start. Coins can only be earned by studying, never bought. Study on five days in all to get the Moon Jelly Crown.

**Works offline, on every device:** your club is kept on this PC, so it works with no connection, and it syncs with your Anvira account when you are online. Signed in at localhousellm.com, open **Account → Study** to see the same Pochi, shop and coins in your browser.
