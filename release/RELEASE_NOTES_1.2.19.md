### Faster AI, same quality
Measured on a laptop with an RTX 3050 and the Qwen2.5-Coder 3B model. The quality checks keep the same number of questions and cards as before.

- **Lessons and mock exams up to 43% faster on a GPU.** Anvira Runtime can run several requests on the graphics card at once, and Anvira now uses that: a lesson's sections are written side by side instead of one after another. Eight sections went from 39 s to 22–26 s.
- **Mock exams are marked faster,** and **mind maps of long notes are built faster,** the same way.
- **The first answer in a notebook chat starts right away.** Your notes are now read once, while the suggested questions load, instead of again when you ask. On a computer without a graphics card the wait before the first word went from about 12 s to under half a second for a few pages of notes.
- Computers without a graphics card keep sending one request at a time, which our tests showed is fastest there.
