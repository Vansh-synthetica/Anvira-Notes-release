### New: Physics Lab
Open it from Study, next to the Chemistry Lab.
- **Solve any problem.** Type a physics problem in English or Tagalog ("A ball is thrown at 20 m/s at 45° from a 15 m cliff…" or "Inihagis ang bato nang 25 m/s…"). The lab reads the numbers and units, solves it step by step with every formula shown, and draws the problem as a live simulation. Move any value and the steps, the answers and the simulation all update. The math is done in the app itself, so the answers are always exact, even without an AI model.
- **Mechanics:** projectiles (with air resistance), straight-line motion, forces and friction on a slope, collisions, springs, pendulums, circular motion, orbits, and a sandbox with real physics for stacking, throwing and knocking things over.
- **Waves & optics:** travelling, superposed and standing waves, the Doppler effect and sonic booms, ray diagrams for lenses and mirrors, refraction and total internal reflection, and single and double slits.
- **Electricity & magnetism:** a circuit builder (batteries, resistors, bulbs, switches, capacitors), electric fields you can drag charges around in, and magnetic fields of wires, solenoids and moving charges.
- **Thermal & modern physics:** an ideal gas made of moving particles, gas processes on a P–V diagram, heating water through melting and boiling, radioactive decay, and the photoelectric effect.

### New: Tagalog
- **Study in Tagalog.** In Settings → Study, set **Language / Wika** to Tagalog and the Study screens switch to Tagalog.
- **The AI answers in your language.** Ask in Tagalog and Pochi answers in Tagalog. Quizzes, flashcards, mock exams, study guides and mind maps can be made in Tagalog, even from English notes: pick the language in the lesson maker, or set **AI writes in** in Settings.
- **Tagalog PDFs and photos are read too.** OCR now recognises Tagalog text.
- For the best Tagalog, use the Gemma 3 4B model (Settings → AI & Models).
